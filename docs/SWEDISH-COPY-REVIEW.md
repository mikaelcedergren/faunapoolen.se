# Swedish language review — 3 October 2026

The owner requested correct, plain Swedish for ordinary readers across the public website,
with existing SEO wording preserved. The review covers the 749 Swedish catalogue entries:
main pages, regional pages, 22 guides, navigation, forms and their feedback, cookie information,
image descriptions, article summaries and search metadata. The private studio has English
interface copy and was not translated as part of this review.

## Corrections

The changes correct grammar and direct translations, explain technical expressions in everyday
language, and make instructions concrete. For example, a visit fee is deducted from the
construction cost, not “the installation”; water samples are taken, not “made”; and readers
are told how to reach equipment for maintenance instead of being asked to interpret “åtkomst”.
Prices, calculations, qualifications, service scope and customer quotations retain their meaning.

All search titles, descriptions, keyword fields and Open Graph wording remain unchanged.
No article addresses, link destinations, image addresses, canonical URLs, language alternates,
redirects or indexing rules are changed by this language review.

## Protected guides

Both protected article bodies and all protected metadata remain untouched. The sole additional
exception is removal of the duplicated final full stop in the Swedish DIY guide introduction:
“misstagen..” becomes “misstagen.” No words change. `expectedGuideIntro` permits only that
specific correction; both historical fixtures remain unchanged. Existing approved exceptions
continue to be checked independently.

## Review evidence

The local website was captured at 1280 × 900 before and after the corrections across all
36 Swedish canonical pages and the not-found page. Screenshots and extracted page text are
kept in ignored `.run/swedish-copy-review/`. No real enquiry was submitted.

The language review also compares every search field, link destination, image address,
translation placeholder and protected field with the pre-review catalogue. The only permitted
protected-field difference is the single full stop above. Production publication is separate.

## Contact wording follow-up

The owner subsequently chose “Kontakta oss” for the former “Be om rådgivning” actions.
Reader-facing instructions using “be om” or “be att” now use direct, contextual wording,
such as “gå igenom”, “ta reda på” and “kontakta installatören”. The nature-pools search
description receives the same contact wording correction; its subject and key terms remain.
Other SEO metadata and all protected guide fields remain unchanged by this follow-up.
Reported municipal requests using “ber” remain factual descriptions, not visitor prompts.
