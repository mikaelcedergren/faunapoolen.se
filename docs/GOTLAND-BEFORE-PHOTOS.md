## Geological background

The regional description was checked on 25 October 2026 against
[SGU’s geological overview of Gotland, report 2021:27, page 8](https://resource.sgu.se/dokument/publikation/sgurapport/sgurapport202127rapport/s2127-rapport.pdf)
and [Länsstyrelsen Gotland’s geological overview](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/gotland/miljo-och-vatten/fororenade-omraden/lanets-arbete-med-fororenade-omraden/forutsattningar-for-arbetet-med-fororenade-omraden.html).
Gotland’s bedrock includes limestone and marlstone; thin soils and exposed rock occur across
large areas, while other areas have deeper, often clay-rich glacial deposits. The public copy
uses the regional limestone and thin-soil context without identifying this property's precise
soil or underlying rock. The site's very hard ground comes from the owner's account. No
site-specific depth, excavation method, drainage claim or exposed bedrock is inferred.
