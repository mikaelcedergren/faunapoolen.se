## Heating guide: reviewed 3 October 2026

- https://gb.bio.top/faq — confirms heating is possible for a specific biological system. Its 28°C maximum is product-specific and is not a Faunapoolen specification.
- https://www.cfgroup.se/cf-naturpool-poolpaket/ — a heat pump is an option on a specific nature-pool offering; not evidence that it is included in Faunapoolen packages.
- https://www.energy.gov.au/households/household-guides/outdoor-living-guide — covers reduce evaporation and heating demand. No savings percentage is transferred to nature pools.
- https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/PH_FR_TSD_2023-03-31.pdf — heat-pump capacity and COP depend on air temperature, humidity and water temperature.
- https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/Pool%20efficiency__0.pdf — evaporation and heat-pump efficiency principles.

The guide makes no universal water-temperature, heating-price or extended-season guarantee. Heating suitability, circulation, cover compatibility and frost protection are assessed for the actual installation. Existing editorial photography illustrates the setting, not an installed heating system.

## DIY and comparison guides — reviewed 5 November 2026

Applies to `src/app/site/articles/build-your-own-nature-pool.ts` and the revised decision checklist in `src/app/site/articles/difference-between-normal-pool-and-natural-pool.ts`, including their seven translations.

- [Aquascape recreational ponds](https://www.aquascapeinc.com/recreational-ponds) — mechanical intake, pumps, wetland filtration, planting, liner protection and maintenance access support the explanation of coordinated systems. Supplier marketing language about chemical-free water or health is not used as evidence of a health benefit or bathing-water safety.
- [Boverket: fixed swimming pools](https://www.boverket.se/sv/PBL-kunskapsbanken/regler-om-byggande/tomter/fasta-bassanger/) — current Swedish drowning-protection guidance. Do not substitute an older municipal depth threshold for this guidance or imply that a small or shallow pool is automatically exempt.
- [Elsäkerhetsverket: building and renovating](https://www.elsakerhetsverket.se/privatpersoner/din-elanlaggning/bygga-och-renovera/) and [ordering electrical installation services](https://www.elsakerhetsverket.se/yrkespersoner/aga-en-elanlaggning/bestalla-elinstallationstjanster/) — a company registered for the relevant Swedish work, clear responsibility, scope and documentation. The DIY guide scopes this advice to Sweden and directs projects elsewhere to their own local checks.

The DIY rewrite does not claim that every system needs equal swimming and regeneration areas, that a fixed maturation period proves bathing quality, or that one material-price minimum applies to every self-build. Clear-looking water is not presented as proof of bathing quality. The comparison checklist asks suppliers to explain their actual filtration, care, costs and responsibilities; it introduces no quantified cost or lifespan claims. The owner-approved site-wide cost and care estimates were retained separately at the owner's request.

The older Aquascape contractor article at `https://www.aquascapeinc.com/professionals/blog/contractor-articles/the-most-effective-way-to-filter-pond-water` returned no readable content during this pass. Its historical 3 October reference is not renewed as checked evidence for this revision.

## Water-use copy — reviewed 5 November 2026

Applies to `src/app/site/sections/nature-pool-benefits.component.ts` and its translations.

- [Aquascape: common pond-water problems](https://www.aquascapeinc.com/water-gardening/how-to/common-pond-water-problems-and-simple-ways-to-fix-them) — supports removing the unconditional promise that water never needs changing. The replacement describes recirculation and makes filling, topping up and any replacement dependent on the actual installation, water conditions and care plan.

Garden-pond instructions are not a complete swimming-water safety standard. This source does not establish a universal water-change interval or justify transferring a pond treatment directly to every swimming installation.

## Danish terminology — reviewed 5 November 2026

- [Svømmesø.dk](https://xn--svmmes-cyae.dk/) and [CEM Pool: biopools and naturpools](https://cempool.dk/biopools-naturpools) — primary examples of Danish terminology. The existing Danish definition now includes `svømmesø` alongside `naturpool`, while explaining that names do not specify a system's design.

This is terminology evidence, not a measured keyword-volume or ranking comparison. Svømmesø.dk identifies itself as a professional archive and says Junckerhaven is not accepting new work; do not present it as a currently available installer without checking again.
