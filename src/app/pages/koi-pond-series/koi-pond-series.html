Aquascape's history and inspiration

Why Aquascape 1000 pond skimmer sets the standard.

Keeping your pond clear and balanced starts at the surface. The Aquascape Signature Series 1000 Pond Skimmer is the most advanced skimmer in Aquascape's range – built to keep the water crystal clear and protect the pump year-round.

The skimmer draws in debris from the water's surface – leaves, pollen, and twigs – before it sinks and decomposes. This means less sludge, less algae, and more time to enjoy the pond instead of cleaning it.
